Summary: Ext^2(I,I) = 0 at every Cohen-Macaulay germ, whether or not it
is a complete intersection, whether or not it is reduced, and whether or
not it is irreducible; and Ext^2(I,I) != 0 at the germs that are not
Cohen-Macaulay.  The reason is Auslander-Buchsbaum: a codimension two
germ is Cohen-Macaulay exactly when pdim R/I = 2, that is pdim I = 1,
and Ext^q(I,-) vanishes for q above the projective dimension of I.
So the germ comparison of Lemma 14.108 can decide only at a point where
the support fails to be Cohen-Macaulay.
