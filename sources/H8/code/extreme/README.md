# Extended runs

The scripts here push verification items of the paper to parameters well
beyond those that `verify_all.py` checks, as a stress test of statements the
paper proves for every `n` but checks only for small `n`. They are not part
of `verify_all.py`, because some of them run for half an hour or more. Their
unedited transcripts are in `transcripts/`. Every run is exact, either over
`Q`, `Q(i)`, `Q(sqrt(-d))`, or modulo a prime `q` in which `-d` is a square
with both square roots imposed. A rank modulo `q` is at most the rank over
the field, so a kernel computed modulo `q` is an upper bound. Each such bound
here meets a lower bound that is known independently: the Weil plane, or
zero.

The three new items of round 11 have `--extreme` modes of their own, in the
directory above:

    python3 ../p2prime.py --extreme --nmax=10   # (LII), exact to n = 10
    python3 ../p2prime_profile.py --extreme     # (LIII), n = 4 in every degree
    python3 ../descent.py --extreme             # (LIV), seven CM fields

Their transcripts are `transcripts/p2prime_extreme.log`,
`transcripts/profile_extreme.log` and `transcripts/descent_extreme.log`, with
run times of 142 minutes, 4.5 minutes and 1.5 minutes. The first reaches
`n = 10` on all five shapes, where HT^2 has dimension 780 and the Chern
character has 2^20 + 2 terms; it was made before item (G) was added to
`p2prime.py`, so its default part shows 35 checks where the script now runs
39, and the extreme part is unchanged.

Run times on one core, from the transcripts: `hh_annihilator_ext.py 6` about
two minutes; `criterion_shape_ext.py` about four minutes; `weil_tori_ext.py`
parts `V`, `A`, `C`, `F`, `D` about 8 s, 26 min, 14 min, 10 min and 3 min;
`weil_tori_B.py` modes `base`, `n5`, `two`, `balanced` about 43 s, 3 min,
2 min and 38 min.

Two sentences of the paper were found wrong by these runs and have been
corrected: item (LI)(D) said that both `alpha_+ ^ (kappa + beta)^n` and
`alpha_- ^ (kappa + beta)^n` become nonzero once `beta` in `wedge^2 V_-` is
added, but only the first does; and the dimension formula of
`thm:hhsplitting`(iv) was stated for every `k`, but it holds for `k >= 1` and
gives `-1` at `k = 0`, where the annihilator is zero. No theorem, lemma or
corollary was contradicted.
