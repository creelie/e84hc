\title[When is every rational Hodge class algebraic?]{When is every rational
Hodge class algebraic?}

\author{Deep Bhattacharjee}
\address{Formerly, Electro-Gravitational Space Propulsion Laboratory (EGSPL),

\email{d.bhattacharjee@erl-forschung.de}

\subjclass[2020]{14C30, 14C25, 14K22, 14J70}
\keywords{Hodge conjecture, motivated cycles, Lefschetz standard conjecture,
Fermat varieties, CM abelian varieties, Weil classes, semiregularity}
\date{October 10, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
We determine which combinations of the standard statements around the
rational Hodge conjecture imply it. Every such combination contains the
conjecture modulo abelian varieties, unless it contains both the Lefschetz
standard conjecture and Andr\'e's conjecture that every Hodge class is
motivated, a pair equivalent to the Hodge conjecture itself. Granting the
recently claimed Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, we prove it for
every Fermat variety and reduce the abelian case to propagation from CM
points. For Weil classes we prove a criterion by bounded degree at CM
points, and we extract from Schoen's cycles one subvariety whose
semiregularity would settle the split Weil families over
$\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$ in every dimension. Without any hypothesis, we prove the Hodge
conjecture for Fermat fourfolds of every odd degree; for degrees prime to six
this rests on a classification of their Hodge characters that is also
checked in Lean. The Hodge conjecture itself is not proved.
\end{abstract}
