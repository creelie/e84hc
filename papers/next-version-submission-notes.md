## Abstract in TeX

```latex
We prove the Hodge conjecture for the Fermat varieties of degrees $44$, $51$,
$52$ and $114$ in every dimension, and more generally of every degree
$2^{a}3^{b}11^{c}$, every degree $2^{a}3^{b}13^{c}$ and every degree
$2^{a}3^{b}19^{c}$ or $2^{a}3^{b}17^{c}$ with $a\le1$, together with their
products and the abelian varieties of Fermat type of these degrees. The key
step is that the quotient of a Fermat threefold by the kernel of a suitable
character is uniruled, by Batyrev's criterion for toric hypersurfaces; a
second proof uses families of curves and a residue formula for their
Abel--Jacobi maps. We also give a profile-descent proof of the Hodge
conjecture for Fermat fourfolds of every odd degree, and determine which
combinations of the standard conjectures around the Hodge conjecture imply
it.
```
